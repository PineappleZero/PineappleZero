## About me / 关于我

I'm a first-year student in **Nanomaterials and Technology at the School of Science, Minzu University of China (MUC), Class of 2026**, with a growing interest in the **intersection of materials science and computing**.

我是**中央民族大学 2026 级理学院 纳米材料与技术专业**本科生，正在把兴趣逐步聚焦到**材料科学与计算的交叉地带**。

What pulls me in is the whole chain from a raw idea to something that actually works: understanding why a material behaves the way it does, writing the code that proves or automates it, and then making the result readable as an image or a film. I use **code, AI, and automation as leverage** — anything that removes repetitive work and buys back time for thinking and making.

吸引我的是一条完整的链条：从最初的想法，到真正能跑起来的东西。先理解一种材料为什么这样表现，再写代码去验证或自动化它，最后让结果以图像或影像的形式被看懂。我把**代码、AI 与自动化当作杠杆**——任何能减少重复劳动、把时间还给思考与创作的东西。
